
### **Edupay - Revolutionizing School Payments with Blockchain**  

![Edupay Logo](/edupay.png)  

---

## 🚀 **Introduction**  
Edupay is a **blockchain-powered school payment solution** built on **Base**. It simplifies **tuition payments, fee tracking, and financial management** for schools, parents, and students by leveraging **secure, transparent, and low-cost transactions**.  

With Edupay, we eliminate inefficiencies in traditional school payment systems by offering **real-time payment tracking, decentralized finance (DeFi) integrations, and automated receipts**, ensuring a **seamless experience** for all stakeholders.  

---

## 🎯 **Problem Statement**  
Traditional school payment systems suffer from:  
✅ **Delays & lack of transparency** – Banks & mobile payments have slow processing times.  
✅ **High transaction costs** – Hidden fees make tuition payments expensive.  
✅ **Limited accessibility** – Unbanked parents struggle to make direct school payments.  
✅ **Manual reconciliation** – Schools manually track payments, leading to errors.  

**Edupay solves these challenges** by offering an **on-chain, automated, and accessible** payment system.  

---

## 🔑 **Key Features**  
✅ **Seamless Crypto Payments** – Pay tuition securely using ETH, USDC, or other Base-supported assets.  
✅ **Real-Time Payment Tracking** – Schools and parents can monitor transactions instantly.  
✅ **Decentralized Fund Management** – On-chain wallets for transparent financial records.  
✅ **Low Transaction Fees** – Powered by Base’s scalable Layer 2 technology.  
✅ **Automated Payment Confirmations** – Instant receipts & audit-friendly reports.  
✅ **Fiat-to-Crypto On-Ramp** *(Coming Soon)* – Easily convert fiat into crypto for school payments.  
✅ **Smart Contract Security** – Transactions are immutable, fraud-proof, and accessible via BaseScan.  

---

## 🏗️ **How It Works**  
1️⃣ **User Connects Wallet** – Parents/students link their **MetaMask, Coinbase Wallet, or WalletConnect**.  
2️⃣ **Selects Payment Plan** – Users enter the amount & select **ETH or USDC** as the payment method.  
3️⃣ **Confirms Transaction** – Payment is processed **on-chain via Base**, with gas fees optimized.  
4️⃣ **Real-Time School Dashboard Update** – Schools instantly track received payments.  
5️⃣ **Receipts & Reports Generated** – Transactions are recorded securely and can be verified.
